# 📊 Blinkit Power BI Dashboard

## Dashboard Overview

This interactive Power BI dashboard provides insights into Blinkit's grocery sales, outlet performance, product categories, and customer preferences.

---

## Dashboard KPIs

- Total Sales
- Average Sales
- Average Rating
- Total Items

- Item Fat Content

---

## Key Insights

- Supermarket Type1 generated the highest sales.
- Tier 3 outlets contributed the largest share of revenue.
- Regular Fat products outsold Low Fat products.
- Fruits & Vegetables and Snack Foods were the top-performing categories.
- Average customer ratings remained consistently high across outlet types.
