# 📈 Business Insights

## Key Findings

### 1. Sales Performance
- Total sales were generated from 8,524 grocery product records.
- Sales varied across different outlet types and locations.

### 2. Item Fat Content
- Regular Fat products generated higher sales than Low Fat products.

### 3. Outlet Performance
- Supermarket Type1 outlets achieved the highest overall sales.
- Grocery Stores contributed comparatively lower sales.

### 4. Outlet Location
- Tier 3 outlets contributed significantly to total revenue.
- Tier 1 and Tier 2 locations showed moderate sales performance.

### 5. Item Categories
- Fruits & Vegetables ranked among the highest-selling item categories.
- Snack Foods also contributed significantly to total revenue.

### 6. Customer Ratings
- Average customer ratings remained consistently positive across outlet types.

---

# Business Recommendations

- Increase inventory for high-selling product categories.
- Focus marketing campaigns on Tier 3 locations.
- Improve sales performance of Grocery Stores.
- Promote Low Fat products to increase customer awareness.
- Optimize stock planning using sales trends.
